before and after the four registered old_a/new_a/new_b/old_b positions.

re-evaluates the registered recipe and common inputs, and compares the complete

not sufficient evidence. Common identities must repeat across all positions.

retry. Completion remains ORDERED_COMPONENTS_RECORDED_PENDING_GATES.

native context absence, GPU engagement, launch and release clearance remain
separate gates. No launch CLI or rental authorization is added.

startup verification or add CLI options. The raw source gate remains separate
from a future reviewed offline decoder/loader. The frozen native dataset calls
still have no revision argument; they have not been changed.
