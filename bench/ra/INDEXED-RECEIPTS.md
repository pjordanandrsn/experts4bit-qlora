# Standalone indexed-copy receipts

This unwired wrapper owns the reviewed indexed collection through complete
five-file Qwen or seven-file Granite exports and known-owned close. Eight fixed
index responses bracket capture. The fixed schema-1 manifest contains only
`schema`, `input_spec`, `stage`, `input_lock`, `input_lock_sha256`, and
`expected_parent`; registered common inputs derive model, revision and pins.
The selected Linux child requires `-I -S -B` and inherited SIGKILL readback before
any index request or sealed allocation. Invoke with `--manifest` and `--out`.
A fresh output contains exactly eight raw responses, five or seven `copy-*`
files, and `result.json` (14 or 16 regular files). Failure prefixes are retained
without retry, including capture/close errors and post-output input drift.

The pure `retained` evaluator requires an independently verified OWN process,
expected argv, original guard evidence, zero return, reap and group absence.
It rehashes every raw response and exported copy, reparses complete indexes,
checks ordinary Git/LFS identities and declared configuration, repeats current
common/source/helper inputs, and compares the complete typed result. Expected
argv and process/transport/reference premises can be forged; this evaluator
does not authenticate them. A caller must establish those premises separately.

Kernel seal, capture descriptor and close snapshots are strictly checked
**child-reported observations**. JSON cannot independently attest past kernel
state or cleanup. Exported regular copies are not still sealed. Their byte and
index equality does not prove an original asset-path read, actual tokenizer,
class/site/extension authentication, full token regeneration, native consumer,
GPU or launch authority. No constructor, worker inventory or ABBA wiring is
added. The baseline, registered instruments and battery remain unchanged.
