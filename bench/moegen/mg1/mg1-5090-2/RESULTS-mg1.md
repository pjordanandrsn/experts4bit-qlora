## Decision (the registration's rule)

Each family whose fused arm PASSES with clean identity checks and P1/P2 engagement enters
`training_support.by_model_type.<type>.fast_train = supported` with its claim ids, and joins `model_families`.
- **Enter on this read:** `lfm2_moe`, `granitemoehybrid`, `ernie4_5_moe` and `nemotron_h`.
- **Enters as `experimental` until P2 is read:** `qwen3_5_moe` (Qwen3.6-35B-A3B). Its fused arm PASSES, with the fused forward
  engaged on all 40 layers.
  - The rule requires P2 engagement, and P2's counter was not recorded on this box: tp1's arm driver predates `DGRAD_STATS`, and
    the ladder OOMed.
  - The timing is consistent with the dgrad kernel. A per-expert dgrad loop over 40 layers would cost seconds per step, the
    loop-based reference arm takes 9.24 s, and the fused arm took 0.377 s. That is inference, not the registered counter.
  - The A2000 slice ladder of the same layers (`bench/moegen/receipts/qwen36_4L_ladder.json`) read dgrad loop 0, on another
    card.
  - MG1 amendment 2 reads the counter on a 5090 at the licensed configuration (r 8, fp32 adapters, the `fused` rung only).
- **Re-cite MG1 as current evidence:** `olmoe` and `gemma4_text`, already supported.
