### Added

- `engines/offload.py`: `hold_forward_residency(handles)`, an opt-in context in which the expert-offload evict post-hook keeps a layer staged after its forward. A layer-major driver can then run every micro-batch through one staging of each layer: a held layer-major step stages 2n layers from cold and 2n - 1 in steady state, against M x (2n - 1) for M micro-batches run one at a time. The single resident slot is unchanged (staging the next layer still evicts the previous one), the exit evicts the resident layer, and routed handles, inference prefetch, a pipelined takeover, a nested hold and a call inside a backward are refused. Default off: no caller enters it yet. Tests: `tests/test_offload_hold.py` (both checkpoint kinds).
