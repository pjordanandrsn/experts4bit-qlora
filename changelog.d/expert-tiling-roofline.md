### Added

- `bench/expert-tiling/`: an analytic note, from existing records, on streaming routed experts host-to-device in sub-expert weight tiles against whole layers, routed whole experts and layer-major. Tiles move no fewer bytes, and under today's layer-level recompute they add 50 %. A forward cannot prefetch the next layer's routed experts, because its routes come from this layer's output. The best schedule without an intra-layer pipeline is therefore layer-major with whole-layer prefetch in the forward and routed prefetch in the recompute (1.068 of the byte floor for a Q235 Shape B pass at 21.76 GB/s). Per-expert pipelining inside a layer reaches 1.010; sub-expert tiles would add under 0.5 %. Documentation and one CPU script; no runs.
