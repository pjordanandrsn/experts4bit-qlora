# Audit: RTX A2000 timings presented as speed evidence (2026-10-05)

*Record, unanchored. Companion audit: grouped-nf4-gemm `docs/audits/a2000-timing-2026-10-05.md`. Line numbers are
`origin/main` at `a447e25`, before this audit's own edits.*

**The rule audited.** The RTX A2000 in the NAS is a shared production box, so it is a correctness testbed only. The
owner set this policy on 2026-07-27 and re-stated it on 2026-10-05. On the A2000 we take compile and launch checks,
numeric agreement, test suites, runner rehearsals, deterministic allocator peak bytes, and Nsight counter ratios. Every
timing, speed ratio, throughput, and prediction-band basis comes from rented compute on the target card. An A2000 timing
may not seed a prediction, filter a candidate, or appear as speed evidence. No label exempts one: "exploratory",
"informational", "within-box" and "pilot" all count.

**Method.** `git grep -n -i A2000` over `docs/`, `README.md`, `CHANGELOG.md` (Unreleased only), `experts4bit_qlora/`,
every `bench/**` PREREG and RESULTS document, and `docs/claims.json`. Every hit is classed:

- **a**: correctness, bytes, counts, determinism, compile or launch, or the policy itself.
- **a-nt**: an A2000 timing quoted only to show it does not transfer.
- **b**: an A2000 timing presented as a reading, finding, corroboration or comparison.
- **c**: an A2000 timing used as the basis of a band, budget, filter, default or decision.

Four read-only classifiers covered the PREREG and RESULTS records. This session reviewed every b and c in an editable
place itself, and spot-checked the classifiers' c calls (TC1:493, P107:26, P39:16, P66:208-214).

**What could be edited.** README, STATUS, the Unreleased CHANGELOG, code comments, living docs, and register rows whose
headline does not move were fixed in the same pull request. Three kinds of document were never edited:

- **PREREGs.** A registration stands as stamped.
- **RESULTS.** Each belongs to its lane. The fix is an erratum section, following experts4bit-qlora#1128.
- **Anchored documents and released CHANGELOG sections.**

Those are listed in §3 with a proposed action.

## 2. For the owner (not applied)

**Energy rows (README headline).**

- **The rows.** `e4b.train.energy-honest.scoped-a2000` (measured) and `e4b.train.energy-honest.a2000-bnb0502.2026-10-04`
  (measured) are both energy per op on the A2000.
- **Where they are quoted.** README.md:32, 85, 252-254; STATUS:957-972; capabilities.json:34, 111, 115; SOLUTIONS.md:68;
  `solutions/bitsandbytes-moe-load-in-4bit-still-ooms.md`:71, 89; BITSANDBYTES.md:79-81; STORAGE-MODES.md:46; METHODOLOGY
  §10.
- **Why they are a question.** The policy does not name energy. But J/op is NVML power integrated over a timed op loop on
  the shared card, so other tenants' load reaches it the way it reaches a timing.
- **Options.**
  - (i) Keep the rows as scoped.
  - (ii) Retire both rows. The README's "1.2-2.3× energy penalty" sentence and capabilities.json's limitation then move
    with them.
  - (iii) **Recommended:** rerun the same one-projection harness on a rented card (a ~$0.10 microbench) and supersede
    both rows.
